"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party or obtained through providing services, including:

02 Obligations

The Receiving Party shall:

  • Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and disclose it only to employees or contractors with a strict need-to-know who have signed equivalent confidentiality agreements
